## Meredith Bouvier, PhD

My past research looked at how AI and digital platforms shape children's
learning, and at what "age-appropriate" technology actually requires in
practice. I built working prototypes to test those ideas, including
[Baseline](https://baselineframework.dev), a framework for age-appropriate
AI responses in schools.

I'm stepping away from research for now. These repositories are archived
and no longer maintained.
